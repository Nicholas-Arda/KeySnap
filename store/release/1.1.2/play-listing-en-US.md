## Title (30 chars max)

KeySnap: Remap Keys & Automate

## Short description (80 chars max)

Remap the shortcut key or any button, and automate your phone. No root needed.

## Full description (4000 chars max, plain text)

Give every button on your phone a new job.

Double-tap Volume Down for the flashlight. Hold Volume Up for a screenshot. Double-press Power to open the camera. KeySnap turns the physical buttons you already have into shortcuts for the things you do all day.

WHAT YOU CAN MAP
• Volume, power, camera and assistant buttons
• Headset and Bluetooth headphone buttons
• Keys on Bluetooth keyboards and game controllers
• Extra buttons your manufacturer added, even unlabelled ones
Each key can be a single press, a double press or a long press, and one shortcut can chain several.

90 ACTIONS
Flashlight, screenshot, open any app, play/pause, next track, volume, brightness, rotation, Do Not Disturb, Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, back/home/recents, tap and swipe on screen, type text, shell command, HTTP request, open a URL, and more. Actions run one after another, so a single press can do several things.

ONLY WHEN YOU WANT
Add conditions to any shortcut: time of day, day of week, charging, battery level, Wi‑Fi network, Bluetooth device, headphones, dark mode, ringer mode, which app is open, whether music is playing. Or skip the key entirely: an Automation runs by itself the moment its conditions become true.

ADVANCED MODE, NO ROOT
Android hides the power button and many manufacturer buttons from normal apps. Advanced Mode uses your phone's own Wireless Debugging to start a small helper that sees every key and keeps running even when the app is closed in the background. No root required, and turning it off removes the helper.

START AFTER RESTART (OPTIONAL)
Enable Start after restart to bring Advanced Mode back after the first unlock and a Wi-Fi connection, using your existing pairing. It is off by default. Android may ask you to allow Wireless Debugging on the network, and some phones need auto-launch enabled. If Android removes or expires the pairing, set up Advanced Mode again; the daily connection cannot guarantee that pairing stays valid.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No servers. No analytics. KeySnap does not send your shortcuts or key presses to the developer. Requests, messages and sharing that you choose go to the destinations or apps you select. The accessibility service is limited to the system Settings app and cannot read anything you type or any other app's screen. Open source under GPL‑3.

LIGHT, DARK OR SEPIA
Three themes and ten languages.

FREE AND PRO
The free version includes two shortcuts and shows ads. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes both limits. No subscription.

PERMISSIONS
KeySnap asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. Accessibility: to detect button presses and run the tap, swipe and pinch gestures you configure. Location: only for the "connected to Wi‑Fi network" condition, because Android hides the network name otherwise; your position is never read. Calendar: read-only, for the "calendar event in progress" condition. Alarms: for calendar events and time-based automations; exact timing may show an Android alarm icon. Notification access: only for the "notification received" condition and "Reply to notification"; notification content is checked on the phone and never saved or sent. Bluetooth, Do Not Disturb, usage access and modify settings each unlock one group of actions. Run at startup and modify secure settings: only for optional Start after restart. Advanced Mode grants the extra permission to enable Wireless Debugging; disabling the option removes it. USB debugging and Android pairing expiry settings are not changed. Camera is never requested; the flashlight works without it.

Requires Android 11 or newer.
